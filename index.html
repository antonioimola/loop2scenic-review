Scenario Editor — Loop2Scenic Benchmark

All 250 Loop2Scenic cards (text, image and video descriptions), rebuilt as scenarios in the editor; most are also run in CARLA with a scripted ego (see the status table). Every entry shows the benchmark original on the left and the built scenario on the right: the CARLA videos, the editor preview, how CARLA compares with the preview (paths, event times, closest approach), and the exported files (.xosc, route XML, save file).

Result is the automatic check of the CARLA run against the editor's preview; the verdict buttons are a human judgement of whether the built scenario shows what the card describes.
